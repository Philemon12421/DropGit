Ludo is one of those games almost everyone has played on a kitchen table: roll the dice, race four tokens around the board, and try to send your opponents back to base. **Ludoka** is a browser-based version of the game, built by Drenchack Tech Company, that runs on any phone or computer without an app store download.

You can try it at [ludoka.vercel.app](https://ludoka.vercel.app/).

## A quick refresher on Ludo

Ludo is played by two to four people. Each player has four tokens that start in a base. The basic ideas are:

- Roll the die; a six is usually needed to bring a token out of the base.
- Move tokens around the track by the number rolled.
- Landing on an opponent's token can send it back to its base.
- The first player to bring all four tokens to the finish wins.

Families around the world play slightly different house rules, which is why many versions of the game exist.

## Ways to play in Ludoka

- **Play vs Computer.** Play single-player against computer opponents. A Milestones progression unlocks harder levels as you win.
- **Pass & Play.** Two to four people share one device and take turns, which is ideal for family time or when friends are in the same room.
- **Play Online.** The game includes a room-based online area with quick match, room creation and joining, avatars and colors. At the time of writing, the site notes that real-time online matches are still being built, so check the game for the current status.

## Two rule sets

Ludoka offers **Classic Ludo** and an **Africa Ludo** variation. According to the game's own description, Africa Ludo changes the feel of a match with rules such as no safe squares, walls formed when tokens are stacked, and the ability to move backward. If you grew up with a house version of the rules, this mode may feel familiar, and it makes games more tactical.

## Progress, leaderboard and the market

Signed-in players can take part in ranked games, earn points and climb through tiers, from Beginner up to Super Ludoka. There is also a referral system for inviting friends. A built-in **Market** lets you spend coins on avatars, themes and boards, and rooms are described as free to join.

## Works like an app

Ludoka can be installed as a Progressive Web App and works offline, so you can play against the computer on a bus or with a weak connection. Installing is done from your browser's menu: look for "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".

## Tips for beating the computer

1. **Do not rush every token out.** Keep a couple of tokens near each other so you can protect them.
2. **Hunt carefully.** Capturing sends an opponent back, but leaving your token exposed to their next roll can cost you more.
3. **Count the distance.** Before you move, check which opponent tokens are within one die roll behind your token.
4. **Learn the rules of the mode.** In Africa Ludo, with no safe squares and backward moves, defensive habits from Classic may not hold.
