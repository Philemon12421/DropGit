## Bugs and feature requests

Open an issue on our [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/Philemon12421/DropGit/issues). Please include the link you tried, what you expected, and what happened instead. Never post private tokens or credentials.

## Questions about using DropGit

Check the FAQ on the [home page](/) and our [blog guides](/blog) first. If your question is not answered there, open an issue and we will reply as soon as we can.

## Privacy and data requests

DropGit does not require an account and does not keep your downloads on our servers. For any privacy question, please read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and then contact us using the options on this page.

## Copyright or abuse reports

DropGit does not host repository content. It fetches files that are publicly available on GitHub and GitLab. If you believe content is infringing your rights, please report it to the hosting platform where it is published. If you believe DropGit itself is being misused, contact us with the details and we will look into it.

## Business and partnership enquiries

DropGit is a product of Drenchack Tech Company. Please contact us through the options above and mention that your message is a business enquiry.
