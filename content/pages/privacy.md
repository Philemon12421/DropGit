This Privacy Policy explains what information DropGit ("we", "us") handles when you use https://dropgit.vercel.app. DropGit is a product of Drenchack Tech Company.

## The short version

- You do not need an account to use DropGit.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- Your download history and theme preference are stored in your own browser, not on our servers.
- The links you paste are processed to fetch public files; we do not keep the downloaded content.

## Information we process

**Links and file requests.** When you paste a GitHub or GitLab link, your browser sends it to our server so we can read the public repository information and fetch the files you select. The server validates the link, retrieves the data from GitHub or GitLab and returns it to you. We do not use this to build user profiles and we do not store the contents of the files you download.

**Technical logs.** Our hosting provider (Vercel) and, where applicable, the platforms we call may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested URL and timestamps for security and operation of the service. These logs are handled under the providers' own policies.

**Information you send us.** If you contact us or open a GitHub issue, we receive whatever you choose to include. Please do not send passwords or access tokens.

## Data stored in your browser

DropGit uses your browser's local storage and service worker cache to remember:

- your download history (stored only on your device; you can clear it in the app),
- your light or dark theme preference,
- your cookie and storage consent choice,
- cached app files so the app can load faster and work offline.

You can clear this data at any time through the History and Settings options in the app or in your browser settings.

## Cookies and advertising

DropGit itself does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels at the time of this policy's last update.

We may in the future show advertisements, for example through Google AdSense. If we do, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and you can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using advertising cookies, and we will update this policy before ads appear.

## Third-party services

- **GitHub and GitLab.** We call their public APIs to read repository information and files. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.
- **Google Fonts.** Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers and Google may receive your IP address.
- **Vercel.** Our site is hosted on Vercel.

## How we use information

We use the information above only to operate and secure DropGit, to fix problems and improve the service, and to respond to messages. We do not sell personal information.

## Security

We validate every link on the server and block requests to private and internal network addresses to protect users and infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect the service.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts, most of your data lives in your own browser and under your control. For other requests, contact us through our [Contact page](/contact).

## Children

DropGit is a general-audience developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when the latest version took effect.
